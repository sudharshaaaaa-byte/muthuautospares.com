OUR STORY

Our Journey Since 2021

Founded in 2021, Muthu Auto Spares & Scrap Yard started with a simple mission — to make quality used auto spare parts affordable and accessible for everyone.

Over the years, we have grown into a trusted destination for vehicle owners, mechanics, and workshops by delivering:

  • Genuine used spare parts
  • Affordable pricing
  • Reliable customer support
  • Quality-tested components

Today, we proudly serve customers across Tamil Nadu through our two physical yards and online order services.
